Getting it here safely

  • The danger zone is 40°F to 140°F. That's where bacteria grow fastest.
  • Hot food stays hot: 140°F or above. Wrap it well in an insulated carrier for the trip. Once you're here, we have a catering hot plate that a Pyrex dish can sit on.
  • Cold food stays cold: 40°F or below. A cooler with ice packs does it.
  • Reheating here? You need to book oven or range space when you sign up, so we can plan around the other dishes. Take it to 165°F. Soups, sauces and gravies to a boil.
  • Two-hour rule: nothing perishable sits out longer than 2 hours.
